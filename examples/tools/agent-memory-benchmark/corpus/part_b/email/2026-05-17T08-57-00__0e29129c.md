Read your prefix-trim spec over the weekend. Direction is good but I have three open questions before I'd sign off on the design:

1. **Trim boundary.** You're trimming on a per-section basis (section headers in `prefix-stable`). Why not per-token? Section-level means a tiny edit to a single section invalidates the cache for that section *and everything before it that re-renders together*. Per-token is more code but the cache hit rate ceiling is higher.

2. **Versioning.** When the trim algorithm itself changes, every cached prefix is invalidated. That's fine, but do we version-pin the algorithm somewhere so a partial deploy doesn't have two workers disagreeing about what to trim?

3. **Backfill interaction.** Backfill items still render with `current_date=ingested_at` (per your last cost-spike fix). Does the trim see them as stable across runs, or does it re-trim every invocation?

Not blocking, just want to think it through before MR opens.
