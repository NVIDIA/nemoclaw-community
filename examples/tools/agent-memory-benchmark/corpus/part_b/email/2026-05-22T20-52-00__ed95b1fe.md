Heads-up on rate-limit changes for chat platform bots, effective **2026-06-15**.

**What's changing**

| Method tier | Old limit | New limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (`chat.postMessage`) | 1 req / sec / channel | unchanged |
| Tier 2 (`conversations.history`, `.replies`) | 50 / min | **30 / min** |
| Tier 3 (`users.lookupByEmail`, `team.info`) | 20 / min | **10 / min** |

If your bot reads thread history at high fan-out, paginate with `cursor` and
respect `Retry-After` on 429s.

**Why:** a small number of bots were holding ~40% of read-API capacity,
which caused latency spikes for everyone else at peak hours.

**What to do**

1. Audit unbounded loops over conversation history.
2. Cache `users.lookupByEmail` results.
3. Test against the new limits in the sandbox starting **2026-06-01**.
