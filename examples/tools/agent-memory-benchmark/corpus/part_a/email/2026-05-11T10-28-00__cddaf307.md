Team,

Heads-up on the shared CI pool — we're splitting the `default` runners into two pools next Monday (May 18).

What changes:
- `pool-fast` — small jobs (lint, unit tests, type-check). 8 cores, no GPU, 4 GB cache.
- `pool-heavy` — anything that builds containers, runs integration suites, or needs the larger cache. 32 cores, 32 GB cache, slower scheduling.

Why: a handful of long-running heavy jobs have been blocking lint pipelines for the rest of the team. The split lets the fast lane stay fast.

What you need to do:
- Default tag stays `default` for now; pipelines auto-route based on declared resource hints.
- If your `.gitlab-ci.yml` already sets `tags:` explicitly, change `default` to one of `pool-fast` / `pool-heavy`.
- The Atlas integration suite will move to `pool-heavy` — Felix will land that MR; no action needed for the Atlas team.

Docs + migration guide: https://wiki.example-co.com/ci/pool-split-2026-05

I'll be in #platform-ci all day Monday for questions.

Felix
