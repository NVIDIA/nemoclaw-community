Good catch, and you're right — I tested this locally with a sleep injected into the wiki writer and the head-of-line blocking is real. The pipeline did stall.

So updated plan:

- Per-item re-queue counter, capped at 3. Default lives in the source config alongside the wave size we discussed.
- After 3, item moves to backfill with a `reason=mutex_contention` marker so the backfill cron can prioritize it over normal slow-drip catch-up.
- Wave dispatch is unblocked the moment the head wave commits, regardless of tail re-queues. Means wave N+1 starts before wave N's stragglers finish.

I think (3) is the more important change actually — it decouples wave latency from item latency, which is what your scenario was really testing.

A couple things I want your read on before I write it up:

(a) Should the backfill cron treat `reason=mutex_contention` items differently from `reason=age`? My instinct is just bump them to the front of the queue, same handler. But arguably they should be retried at a slower cadence — if the mutex was hot, hammering it from backfill doesn't help.

(b) Where do we surface this? I was going to add a counter to `metricswatch` so we can alert if mutex_contention spikes — gives Tomas something actionable when he's on-call. Or is that over-engineering for v1?

I'll push a doc revision tonight either way. If you have a strong take on (a) or (b), reply before then; otherwise I'll pick and we'll iterate in review.
