Wed 2pm, 1.5 hours, on the calendar. Anya's in.

She read your reference doc this morning and is already coming around on the registry model. Her 5 bullets on job-submission friction are below — pasting raw, will tighten before the meeting:

1. Submit + wait is awkward. We want a fire-and-forget option that returns a handle, not blocks.
2. No way to cancel a queued job before it starts running.
3. Job metadata is structurally opaque — we serialize JSON into a TEXT column, querying it later is painful.
4. Retry semantics aren't surfaced. Beacon needs at-least-once for eval; some Atlas jobs need at-most-once.
5. Concurrency caps are global, not per-job-kind.

(4) is the one I expect to be the hardest to align on.
