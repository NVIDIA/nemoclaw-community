Team,

A few of you have asked, separately, whether our 1:1 cadence is still working — frequency, structure, what we cover. Rather than guess, I want to actually hear it from everyone on the team.

I've put together a short anonymous feedback form. Five questions, takes about three minutes:
- Is the current 30-min weekly cadence the right shape for you?
- Are we covering the things you most need to cover (project, growth, escalations, calibration)?
- What do you wish we talked about more? Less?
- Is there a topic you've been avoiding because the format doesn't feel right for it?
- Anything else you want me to see.

Form: https://forms.examplecorp.example/platform-eng-1on1-feedback-2026q2

Two things to be explicit about. First, it really is anonymous — I see aggregates only, no per-respondent attribution. If the cadence needs to change for some of you and not others, that's a fine outcome — happy to run different shapes per person.

Deadline: please respond by EOD Friday May 29. If you want to talk to me directly instead of using the form, that's also fine; pull me into a 1:1 slot and we'll talk.
