Team,

Recap from Thursday's all-hands for those who couldn't make it — the Atlas roadmap themes I committed to in front of the org.

Three themes for the next two quarters:

1. Reliability over surface area. We've been adding features faster than we've been making them durable. The bar for new surface in Q3 is: it ships behind a feature flag, has a rollback story, and a named on-call. If those three aren't true, it doesn't ship.

2. Cost per session. Atlas's per-session compute cost is the single biggest blocker to the next tier of users. Alex's team is leading prompt-prefix-cache adoption and ingest cost reduction; expect concrete targets in the Q3 OKRs.

3. Cross-project leverage. Atlas / Beacon / Compass duplicate work in places we can no longer afford. The shared-runtime convergence with Rohan's team continues; Compass dashboards consume Atlas events directly starting next sprint.

What I'm asking of leads:
- Reflect these themes in your team's plans by end of May.
- If a current project doesn't fit, surface it now, not at the quarter boundary.
