The caller needs pods/get and pods/attach, but not pods/exec. The chart does not
create an end-user RoleBinding.
Assign this client to one trusted operator only. Do not share one client among
mutually untrusted users; deploy separate release/client identities instead.

sandbox or retained Hermes PVC during uninstall. Use OpenShell for reviewed
sandbox lifecycle operations.
